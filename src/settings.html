    How blocking works

    YouTube can also insert ads directly into the video stream. Those cannot be removed by URL rules, so the player helper skips them or races through them. YouTube can change its player at any time.

    If playback feels slow

    YouTube sometimes adds a few seconds of delay and an “Experiencing interruptions?” note when it detects ad blocking. To see whether Seraphtube is involved, turn blocking off on the Ward tab and try a video, or switch to the YouTube Stock theme to rule out theme CSS. The stricter Google ad list is off by default because it is the likeliest to trigger that slowdown.
