    Blocking coverage

    macOS and Linux use WebKit content rules for browser-managed requests. Windows uses the injected JavaScript fallback for fetch, XHR, sendBeacon, YouTube response cleanup, and cosmetic removal. The Windows fallback cannot intercept every browser-managed image, script, iframe, or media request.

    YouTube can also insert ads directly into a video stream. Those cannot be reliably removed by URL rules; the player helper attempts to skip or speed through detected ad segments. YouTube can change its player at any time.
